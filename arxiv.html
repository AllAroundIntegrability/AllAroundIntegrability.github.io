Short summaries of recent preprints by people who took part in the previous editions of the workshop (Modena 2025, Bologna 2026) or who speak at this year's meeting. Names in pink are members of the community. Summaries are our own condensed reading of the abstracts; please see the arXiv for the full papers. Last updated 19 October 2026.

AdS/CFT, QSC and defects

Deriving the AdS3×S3×T4 Quantum Spectral Curve II: T-system and QSC

2610.071475 Oct 2026
A. Cavaglià, D. Polvara, N. Primi, A. Sfondrini, R. Tateo

Second part of the derivation of the QSC for strings on AdS3×S3×T4 with pure Ramond–Ramond flux. Starting from the extended Y-system of part I, the authors build the T-system and arrive at the QSC, confirming the form that had previously been conjectured and finding additional relations among its solutions. They show how massless excitations are encoded in the analytic structure, and the final result is a compact Pμ-system.

Deriving the AdS3×S3×T4 Quantum Spectral Curve I: Y-system and discontinuity relations

2607.1367315 Jul 2026
A. Cavaglià, D. Polvara, N. Primi, A. Sfondrini, R. Tateo

The first step of the programme: the mirror TBA equations for AdS3×S3×T4 superstrings are recast as a Y-system together with its discontinuity relations, specialised to pure-RR backgrounds. This sets up the analytic input needed for the T-system and QSC of the companion paper.

Cusped Defects: Cusp Operator Expansion, Conformal Properties and Bootstrap Applications

2609.040353 Sep 2026
L. Bianchi, A. Cavaglià, A. D. Gutiérrez, S. R. Kousvos, M. Meineri

Shows that suitably defined cusp scaling operators transform like conformal primaries, and that line defects of general shape can be expanded in a basis of cusped defects. The construction is tested on the localized magnetic-field defect, and a crossing equation yields analytic constraints on cusp data that could feed into numerical bootstrap studies.

2D QFT, TBA and ODE/IM

Generalised Gibbs Ensembles from the ODE/IM Correspondence: the Lee–Yang Model

2610.107167 Oct 2026
S. K. Ashok, M. Downing, F. Karimi, A. Sudhakar, R. Tateo

Constructs the ODE side of generalised Gibbs ensembles for the chiral Lee–Yang CFT. The chemical potentials are encoded in a deformed cubic oscillator, fixed order by order so that its WKB period reproduces the driving term of the TBA; the spin-5 ensemble is worked out for two states. The Stokes multiplier obeys the Lee–Yang Y-system, the deformed eigenvalues match an independent CFT computation, and Borel resummation of the ground-state energy agrees with the numerical TBA over a wide range of chemical potential.

Root systems and Minimal RG flows to c=1 CFTs

2610.089386 Oct 2026
C. Ahn, S. Kostadinovic, F. Ravanini

Extends Dorey's root-system construction of exact S-matrices from the massive to the massless case, and checks that the resulting S-matrices satisfy the bootstrap equations. The TBA built from them describes minimal RG flows into the Dn coset CFTs at c=1 (free bosons), and its analysis provides UV completions of these flows.

On the commuting charges and the Bethe ansatz for the quantised Kadomtsev–Petviashvili equation

2609.1531614 Sep 2026
C. Paletta, A. Torrielli

For a recently proposed quantisation of the KP equation, the authors write down the first higher charge H3 and prove that it commutes with the other charges once a specific UV divergence is regularised, independently of the regulator under fairly general assumptions (with AI-assisted proofs under their supervision). They also give the general solution of the two-body problem in the finite-volume Bethe ansatz.

Bethe ansatz and integrable circuits

Yangian Doubles and off-Shell Bethe Vectors

2607.005941 Jul 2026
A. Liashyk, S. Pakuliak, E. Ragoucy

Builds off-shell Bethe vectors for generic 𝔤-invariant integrable models out of the currents of the Yangian doubles of the classical series. The vectors are shown to have the properties used earlier to derive rectangular recurrence relations, and the on-shell eigenvalue property is verified.

Open-boundary integrable quantum circuits with different geometries

2607.020932 Jul 2026
M. García Fernández, C. Paletta, A. L. Retore

Asks which open-boundary quantum circuits of arbitrary geometry are Yang–Baxter integrable. Using transfer matrices with staggered inhomogeneities (plus a new third type), the authors map inhomogeneities and system size to gate arrangements, and conjecture that a time-periodic circuit is integrable when bulk and boundary gates solve the Yang–Baxter and reflection equations and each neighbouring pair receives the bulk gate exactly once per period. They also give a detection algorithm, show a minimal depth of four, and find reflection matrices for 6- and 8-vertex non-difference R-matrices.

Out of equilibrium, transport and entanglement

Transport and thermalization under noncommuting localized constraints

2610.2378116 Oct 2026
A. Nesturov, A. Takács, L. Zadnik

Studies how spatially localised conserved quantities shape dynamics in the integrable quantum Volterra model, its non-integrable classical limit, and two derived cellular automata. The constraints fragment the space uniformly, and inside each quantum fragment the dynamics is exactly a quantum chiral Potts chain. Energy transport is ballistic (with fragment-independent coefficients) in the quantum case and diffusive in the classical one, while in the automata transport is unaffected but local ergodicity breaks down strongly.

Operator-Space Entanglement Entropy in Random Fermionic Gaussian Dynamics

2610.2172315 Oct 2026
P. Bayona-Pena, E. Gramellini, L. Piroli

Computes the time evolution of the operator-space entanglement entropy of initially product operators under Brownian SYK2 dynamics. Mapping the problem to domain-wall melting and using random-matrix tools (Coulomb gas, free Jacobi process), the authors obtain an exact formula in the thermodynamic limit and an analytic description of the late-time Gaussian Page curve.

Quantum Stochastic Walks on the Permutation Group

2609.1338711 Sep 2026
F. He, A. Hutsalyuk, G. Mussardo, A. Stampiggi

Uses card shuffling (random transpositions on the symmetric group) to ask whether quantum coherence can speed up mixing. A permutation Hamiltonian generates a unitary walk that competes with dissipation in a quantum stochastic walk. Coherence can only reduce the distance from the uniform distribution, the slowest mode gives a condition for a sizeable speedup, and the ratio of quantum to classical mixing times shows a one-parameter scaling collapse.

Entanglement Asymmetry in Random Quantum Automata

2607.075568 Jul 2026
O. A. Castro-Alvaredo, D. Szász-Schagrin, M. Mazzoni

Follows the U(1) entanglement asymmetry in ensembles of random quantum automata (basis permutations combined with phases). At late times, 2-local circuits reproduce the all-to-all average and symmetrise locally, but, unlike Haar-random circuits, the symmetrisation scale depends on the initial state; the growth of asymmetry tracks subsystem coherence.

Correlation and entanglement dynamics of free fermions in disguise

2607.023592 Jul 2026
D. Szász-Schagrin, P. Bayona-Pena, L. Piroli, E. Vernier

Applies the post-quench toolbox of integrable systems to spin chains equivalent to "free fermions in disguise", whose spectra are exponentially degenerate. The authors obtain the quasi-momentum distribution of the GGE and expectation values of local observables, and adapt the quasiparticle picture for entanglement. Local observables match tensor-network numerics very well, while entanglement shows small deviations, suggesting the quasiparticle picture is only approximate here.
